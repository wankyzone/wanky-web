# Weekly Publication Guidelines

> Draft for review. Apply these checks to weekly updates and other public progress reports.

## Accuracy

- Separate verified facts from plans, estimates, and work in progress.
- Use status language precisely: “started” or “in progress” is not “complete.”
- Do not announce a launch date, service choice, architecture, capability, or metric unless it has been confirmed and approved.
- Describe validation that remains to be done without implying it has passed.

## Dates and numbering

- Confirm the publication date before publishing.
- Confirm the weekly update number before publishing; otherwise keep it marked “To be confirmed” in the draft.
- Do not infer a date or number from the week in which a draft was written.

## Privacy and approval

- Exclude credentials, private infrastructure configuration, internal customer information, and unapproved internal plans.
- Keep draft status visible during review.
- Obtain approval for the final content and publication timing before treating an update as published.
