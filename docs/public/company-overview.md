## Principles

- **Systems over shortcuts:** consider how products, services, data, and people fit together.
- **Reliability over hype:** validate important workflows and make claims that evidence supports.
- **Security by design:** consider access, credential handling, data responsibility, and risk as part of engineering decisions.
- **Clarity over complexity:** solve the defined problem with an approach appropriate to the need.
- **Progress through evidence:** build, test, observe, and improve based on user needs and technical findings.
